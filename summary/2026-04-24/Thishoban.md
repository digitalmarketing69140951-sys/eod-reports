## CRITICAL OBSERVATION — EOD FORMAT ISSUE
The EOD reports submitted this week are written in a self-evaluating, coaching style ("You focused on...", "You missed...", "What worked:", "What failed:"). This is not an operator EOD. This is an AI-generated or self-critique document masquerading as a work log. A legitimate EOD records: what was done, what was changed in which system, evidence IDs, and what the actual measured outcome was. The format used this week makes it impossible to independently verify what was actually executed versus what was planned or described. This is flagged as a structural accountability failure and is treated as such throughout this audit.

### 7. TOP 3 CORRECTIONS (MANDATORY)

1. **EODs must be written in first person as an operator work log, not as an AI-generated self-assessment.** Format: what I did, what system I changed, what the evidence ID is, what the measured outcome was. No coaching tone, no "you" perspective, no "what worked / what failed" framing that reads like an external review. If AI is being used to generate EODs, this must stop immediately. The EOD is an accountability document, not a performance reflection exercise.

2. **Every EOD must include a campaign-level ROAS and spend table pulled from Google Ads.** Not a trend narrative, not "expected impact" — actual numbers: campaign name, spend today, conversions today, ROAS today. This takes 5 minutes to pull. It was present on Thu and absent on Mon and Wed. It must appear every working day from next Monday.

3. **Zero-impression SKU work must be validated before time is invested.** Before optimising any zero-impression SKU, confirm: (a) the keyword has search volume in Keyword Planner or Search Trends, (b) the product is priced competitively, (c) the SKU is approved in GMC. Document this filter in the EOD. If these checks fail, the SKU is deprioritised and time goes to high-impression, low-CTR or high-spend, low-conversion SKUs instead.
