The search term and product fixes are the right type of action but at insufficient scale. One negative keyword per day and one product optimisation do not match the urgency of a campaign sitting 30 percentage points below target and declining daily. No budget reduction, no bid intervention, no product exclusion, no escalation to management. **This is the biggest missed opportunity of the week.**

ROAS of 267% on £50 spend is not the same as 267% on £500.

For a Campaign 2 that has been in decline for the full week, the search term cleanup rate is too slow. A structured search term audit pulling all high-cost, zero-conversion queries over 14 days would have produced 10–20 actionable negatives in a single session.
