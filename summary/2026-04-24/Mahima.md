### 7. TOP 3 CORRECTIONS (MANDATORY)

1. **Every EOD must open with a campaign-level performance table: campaign name, spend (€), conversions, conversion value (€), and ROAS.** This is non-negotiable for an account owner. SKU-level revenue attribution is valuable but it does not replace the campaign-level view. Pulled directly from Google Ads UI. Must appear Mon–Fri from next week with no exceptions.

2. **Before any leave period, a handover note must be documented in the pre-leave EOD.** This note must name: (a) who is monitoring the account in the operator's absence, (b) any campaigns at risk that need watching, (c) any pending actions that need to be completed. Tue's EOD had none of this. If no coverage exists, that must be escalated to management before the leave starts — not silently assumed.

3. **Scaling decisions must be preceded by a confirmed current ROAS figure for the campaign the SKU lives in.** "Scale 7998061084937" is not a complete instruction. The scaling action must state: current campaign ROAS, target ROAS, current daily budget, proposed budget increase, and expected ROAS impact. If this data is not available at time of writing, the scaling action is deferred until it is.
