### 7. TOP 3 CORRECTIONS (MANDATORY)

1. **Backlinks are non-negotiable — a minimum daily target must be set and met starting Monday.** Zero backlinks for a full week in an SEO role is not acceptable regardless of what else was completed. Starting Monday, backlink outreach or acquisition must appear as a completed task in every EOD. If there is a tool, access, or knowledge blocker preventing this, it must be escalated to the team lead by Monday morning — not carried as a silent omission.

2. **No AI tool experimentation during core working hours until on-page SEO daily targets are met.** Gem creation, merchant data analysis tools, and prompt engineering are secondary activities. They must not consume time until the daily quota of product optimizations, listings, and any assigned blog or backlink tasks are completed and confirmed. AI tooling is a productivity multiplier only when core output is already at target volume.

3. **Establish and report against a fixed daily output target for listings and optimizations — and hold it.** The drop from ~10 products (Tue) to 1 product (Thu/Fri) is not explained and not acceptable. Starting Monday, the EOD must state: target for the day, actual completed, and a reason if target was missed. Without a stated target, there is no accountability baseline and no visibility for the team lead.
