At a Google Ads operator rate, that is a full day of strategic work unexecuted.

- **Price changes made without competitor benchmark or margin validation (Thu flagged explicitly).** Blind pricing adjustments on active Shopping SKUs risk margin erosion and search auction position loss.

### 7. TOP 3 CORRECTIONS (MANDATORY)

1. **Execute at least one scaling action per day when ROAS > target.** If ROAS exceeds 400, budget must be reviewed and increased on the top-performing campaigns or SKU groups. No more weeks where a 455 ROAS signal is held for 5 days without a budget or bid response.

2. **Hard cap: maximum 8 SKUs per day, maximum 3 edit types per SKU (title, product type, price only — no descriptions, no image changes unless CTR data confirms the need).** This is non-negotiable. Descriptions and images are not Shopping/PMax ROAS drivers without evidence. Every hour saved from over-editing goes into search term mining and campaign structure.

3. **Produce a written campaign-level decision once per week (every Friday).** This must include: current ROAS per campaign, spend vs revenue per campaign, and a clear scale/hold/cut decision for each. No exceptions. This is what transitions the role from feed operator to performance strategist.
