### 7. TOP 3 CORRECTIONS (MANDATORY)

1. **Every EOD must open with 3 numbers: ROAS, total spend, and total conversions for the day — pulled from the platform, not estimated.** No exceptions. No projections. No "expected to improve in 24–72 hours." If these numbers are not in the EOD, the report is incomplete and the day's work cannot be evaluated.

2. **Search term mining executes every day — minimum 30 minutes, non-negotiable.** 4 days of no documented negative keyword work on a live multi-campaign account is unacceptable. It must be the first optimization task every session, before any feed or Shopify work begins.

3. **Every batch of product IDs optimized must include the selection reason.** "These 12 IDs had clicks but no conversions" is acceptable. A list of 12 IDs with no stated reason is not. If you cannot explain why a product was chosen for optimization, it should not be touched.

**Where they converted opportunity into high-value output:**
- Monday's asset group isolation for proven SKUs was the week's best structural decision. Correctly separating winners reduces data dilution and is the right move before scaling budget.
- Tuesday's Klarna exclusions (high spend, zero sales) was direct ROAS protection executed cleanly.
- Monday's price mismatch fix removed a known conversion blocker.

**Where time was wasted:**
- Wed–Fri: Combined ~20 hours of feed and Shopify work across 30+ product IDs with no ROAS anchor, no search term control, and no scaling action. The volume of work is not in question — the absence of measurement makes it impossible to call this productive.
- New listing creation on 3 days — slow-return activity given existing products with traffic signals likely needed attention first.

**Tasks that appeared productive but were low-impact:**
- Bulk 12-ID feed optimization batches (Tue, Wed) — high time cost, no attribution possible, no selection logic documented.
- Wednesday's EOD — written as a success summary with no audit. Describes output, not impact.
- Description enhancements on Shopping/PMax-fed products — low signal weight in Google's auction system.

**Blockers not escalated early enough:**
- No ROAS data across the entire week suggests either the account dashboard is not being checked daily or metrics are not being extracted before writing the EOD. This is a process failure that should have been flagged to the team lead by Wednesday at the latest.
- High-CTR, zero-conversion SKUs (referenced implicitly in the Shopify fine-tuning rationale) were not escalated to the technical or SEO team for landing page review at any point this week.

### 9. FORWARD RISK

**Company risk if pattern continues:**
- Budget is being scaled (Monday's increase) and campaigns are being structurally changed (asset groups, exclusions) without any performance measurement attached. Decisions made without data create compounding errors that are difficult to diagnose and reverse. If ROAS is declining, this week's activity may have accelerated it without anyone noticing.
- 20+ hours of feed work per week without a measurement framework means the effort may be systematically misdirected — optimizing products that don't convert while ignoring products that could scale.

**Career risk if pattern continues:**
- Self-assessed "strong execution" with no metrics is the fastest way to lose credibility with senior management. When results are reviewed at a campaign level and the EOD trail contains no performance data, the operator appears disconnected from outcomes. This limits advancement to a strategist or account lead role.
- Repeating large batches without selection criteria signals that optimization decisions are made on intuition, not data. On a Germany-market account with language and localization complexity, that is a compounding liability.
