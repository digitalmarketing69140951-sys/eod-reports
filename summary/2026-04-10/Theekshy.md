### 7. TOP 3 CORRECTIONS (MANDATORY)

1. **Every EOD must open with 3 numbers pulled from the Google Ads or Merchant Center dashboard: ROAS, total spend, and total conversions for that day.** Not estimates. Not "ROAS stable." Not "structurally improved." Actual numbers from the platform. This is a daily habit — build it this week and do not skip it once.

2. **Search term review executes every working day — minimum 20 minutes.** Pull the search terms report for the active campaign, identify any irrelevant or high-spend low-conversion query, and add a negative if warranted. Document what was found and what was done. Even if nothing is actioned, "reviewed search terms — no new negatives required" is a valid entry. Silence is not.

3. **Every optimisation action must have a follow-up validation entry within 5 working days.** If products were optimised Tuesday, Friday's EOD must include the CTR delta for those specific products. Write the validation check into the next-day task list at the time of optimisation — not as an afterthought. "Monitor for 3–4 days" without a calendar entry and a follow-up record is not a closed loop.

### 9. FORWARD RISK

**Company risk if pattern continues:**
At junior level, the risk is primarily directional — working on the right type of task (conversion optimisation, product fixes, audience signals) but without the measurement infrastructure to confirm direction. As account complexity increases and SKU volume grows, this gap becomes more expensive. Optimising products without checking whether previous optimisations worked means decisions compound without validation — the account's direction becomes harder to diagnose.

**Career risk if pattern continues:**
The analytical thinking visible in this week's EODs is a genuine strength. The risk is that it develops faster than the operational habits (data pull, search term mining, validation closure) that make the analytical work credible and scalable. In a performance review context, strong thinking with no supporting data is less valuable than moderate thinking with clean measurement. Both need to develop in parallel.
