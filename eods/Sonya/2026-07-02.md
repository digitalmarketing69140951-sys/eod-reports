Audience Segmentation Strategy

Created two separate GA4 audience segments to isolate suspicious high-volume traffic.

The primary audience targets users generating more than 40 page views within a rolling 30-day period, while the second audience captures additional levels of abnormal engagement.

This approach avoids excluding genuine customers who naturally perform extensive product research before purchasing.

30-Day Validation Plan

Instead of immediately excluding these audiences from advertising campaigns, established a structured 30-day observation period to monitor:

Transactions
Assisted conversions
Returning visitors
Delayed purchases
Long buying journeys

This ensures any future exclusions are based entirely on verified performance data.
