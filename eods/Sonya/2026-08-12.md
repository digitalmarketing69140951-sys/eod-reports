# End of Day (EOD) Report

**Date:** August 12, 2026


## 1. Work Summary

After completing my daily PPC monitoring and optimization routine, I dedicated approximately **4 hours to a detailed PPC + Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) impact analysis**.

The main objective was to solve a key performance problem: **products receiving paid traffic but failing to convert despite generating 20+ clicks**.

Rather than treating these products as purely PPC problems, I analyzed the complete conversion funnel:

**Google Ads → Search Intent → Merchant Center Feed → Product Listing → Landing Page → User Behaviour → Purchase**

I used Microsoft Clarity behavioural data, Google Ads performance data and historical search-query data to identify the actual reasons behind poor conversion performance.

The session resulted in improvements across **UK PPC/CRO, US Merchant Center expansion, product feed relevance, landing-page clarity, pricing competitiveness and zombie SKU recovery.**

---

# 2. Sales & ROAS Alignment Check

This work was directly aligned with the primary objective of **increasing sales while protecting ROAS**.

Instead of immediately increasing budgets to generate additional traffic, I focused on getting **more value from traffic we are already paying for**.

The optimization strategy addressed three major areas:

### 1. Reduce wasted advertising spend

Products receiving clicks without conversions were investigated to determine whether the issue was targeting, product relevance, pricing or landing-page friction.

### 2. Increase conversion rate

Microsoft Clarity recordings were used to identify where customers were hesitating, becoming confused or potentially abandoning the page.

### 3. Improve Shopping/PMax traffic quality

Historical converting search queries were incorporated into product titles and feed attributes so Google receives stronger signals about the product's actual search intent.

This creates a stronger opportunity to improve:

**CTR → Conversion Rate → Conversion Value → ROAS**

The strategy therefore supports both **short-term ROAS protection and long-term sales growth.**
