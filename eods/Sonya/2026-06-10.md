Main Project 1: Standard Shopping Campaign Compliance Blueprint (4.5 Hours)
Objective
Designed and documented a complete Standard Shopping Campaign SOP and compliance framework to standardize campaign launches, protect ROAS, and improve scalability across the team.
This was not a campaign setup task—it was operational architecture development.

Core Framework Developed
1. 14-Day AI Learning Phase Strategy
Established launch framework:
Initial tROAS between 50%–100%.
Maintain low restriction during learning period.
Scale toward target ROAS after sufficient conversion data accumulates.
Why
High ROAS targets at launch often restrict traffic and prevent learning.
The framework allows Google to:
Gather conversion signals faster.
Build stronger product-level intelligence.
Reach optimization phase more efficiently.
Business Impact
Faster learning cycles.
Reduced campaign stagnation.
More reliable scaling opportunities.

2. Pre-Launch Safety Guardrails
Created mandatory campaign launch requirements:
Conversion Tracking Rules
Purchases only.
Remove secondary conversion goals.
Feed Controls
Explicit feed selection required.
Prohibited use of "All Feeds."
Campaign Settings
High Priority required.
Customer acquisition bid cap limited to 0.10.
Tracking template requirements documented.
Approval Process
Campaigns must remain paused until final approval.
Business Impact
Prevents incorrect launches.
Eliminates tracking contamination.
Protects profit margins.
Improves attribution accuracy.

3. Budget Allocation Framework
Created SKU-based budget controls.
Rule:
£25/day starting budget only permitted for collections exceeding 100 SKUs.
Smaller collections require proportionally reduced budgets.
Why
Small product sets cannot efficiently absorb large budgets.
Business Impact
Prevents budget overspending.
Improves spend efficiency.
Protects launch ROAS.

4. Campaign Naming Standardization
Developed mandatory 7-part naming convention.
Structure:
Type | Member | CSS | Feed Label | Segmentation | Bidding | Country
Why
Creates immediate account clarity.
Business Impact
Faster audits.
Easier troubleshooting.
Simplified reporting.
Improved team scalability.

5. Cross-Channel Optimization Framework
Integrated multiple data sources into campaign management process:
Google Ads Planner
Google Search Terms
Amazon Search Terms
Why
Customer demand signals exist across multiple platforms.
Business Impact
Better keyword discovery.
Improved feed optimization.
Stronger search intent alignment.

Strategic Rationale Behind the Blueprint
The framework was designed to solve four major operational risks:
Risk 1: AI Suffocation
Problem:
Launching campaigns with aggressive ROAS targets limits learning.
Solution:
Lower initial ROAS targets.
Controlled learning period.
Result:
Better algorithm development.
Faster optimization.

Risk 2: Budget Waste
Problem:
Overspending during launch phases.
Solution:
SKU-based budget framework.
Customer acquisition bid restrictions.
Result:
Stronger profitability control.

Risk 3: Data Pollution
Problem:
Multiple conversion actions distort optimization.
Solution:
Purchases-only optimization model.
Result:
Cleaner bidding signals.

Risk 4: Human Error
Problem:
Inconsistent campaign setups across team members.
Solution:
Standardized SOP.
Result:
Consistent execution across all team members.
