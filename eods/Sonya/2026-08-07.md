## Main Work Completed

### 1. Competitor Shopping Benchmark

Reviewed competing products appearing for selected commercial searches. Compared product titles, images, pricing, descriptions and selling points to understand where our listings were stronger or weaker.

The findings were used to identify practical improvements that could increase Shopping competitiveness without making unnecessary changes to products that were already performing well.

### 2. High-AOV Product Analysis

Conducted a deeper review of high-value products that had received clicks but had not generated sales recently.

Instead of automatically excluding them, I considered their price, traffic quality, listing quality and conversion history to determine whether they required CRO/listing improvements or advertising restrictions.

### 3. Product-Level Budget Review

Reviewed where advertising spend was being consumed across products and identified opportunities to concentrate budget on products showing stronger conversion signals.

## Sales & ROAS Alignment Check

The main objective was to balance ROAS protection with sales-growth opportunities. High-AOV products were reviewed carefully because a small number of conversions can create significant conversion value.
