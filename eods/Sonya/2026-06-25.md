2. Monthly Product Recovery Campaign Preparation (3 Hours)

Today I prepared a completely new product group for a dedicated Google Shopping / Performance Max campaign.

The objective was to recover products that previously demonstrated sales potential but had become inactive during the current month.

Product Analysis

I analyzed my allocated product portfolio and identified products that met the following criteria:

Sold more than once during the previous month.
Generated zero sales during the current month.

These products had already proven they could convert customers, making them strong candidates for reactivation rather than abandoning them.

Actions Completed
Identified all qualifying product IDs.
Added dedicated product tags for campaign segmentation.
Created a new Shopify collection containing only these products.
Built a dedicated Google Merchant Center feed linked exclusively to this collection.
Why This Was Done

Instead of allowing previously successful products to remain inactive, I isolated them into a dedicated campaign group where performance can be managed independently.

This approach avoids mixing recovering products with established best sellers while giving them a second opportunity to generate sales.

Business Impact

This creates a structured recovery strategy that:

Re-engages previously successful products.
Improves campaign organization.
Enables dedicated budget control.
Provides cleaner performance measurement.
Creates a scalable workflow for future monthly recovery campaigns.

4. CPPC Phase 2 Dashboard Validation & BGCT Audit (2.5 Hours)

Conducted a comprehensive validation of the Phase 2 CPPC Tasks Grid, comparing the automation dashboard against live Google Ads data and the company's internal Budget Growth Criteria Table (BGCT).

The objective was to verify that the automation produces reliable recommendations before it is used for operational decision-making.

System Audit

Identified several global dashboard issues including:

Missing calendar date selectors.
Missing "Orders 30D" mappings.
Non-functional "Action Taken" checkboxes.
Incorrect EU currency formatting.

These issues reduce dashboard usability and can lead to confusion during campaign reviews.

Task Validation

Audited the following automation rules:

SCALE_WINNER
KILL_LOSS
REDUCE_WASTE

During validation, I discovered major discrepancies where the dashboard reported £0 revenue for campaigns that were actually generating 300%+ ROAS in live Google Ads.

BGCT Rule Validation

Cross-checked dashboard recommendations against the internal Budget Growth Criteria Table.

Discovered that the automation was making decisions based only on isolated 30-day data, while completely ignoring the required 60-day and 90-day BGCT protection rules.

Without these safeguards, the system could recommend:

Scaling the wrong campaigns.
Pausing profitable campaigns.
Making inaccurate optimization decisions.
Workflow Improvement Proposals

Prepared detailed recommendations for the development team.

Major proposals included:

Automation Improvements
Replace manual "Draft Note" prompts with one-click CSV downloads.
Automatically generate exclusion lists for:
60-day zero-conversion products.
90-day low-ROAS products.
User Interface Improvements
Calendar range selectors.
Correct EUR currency labels.
Proper "Orders 30D" mapping.
Functional action tracking checkboxes.
Management Communication

Prepared a structured report for management clearly separating:

API synchronization problems.
Conversion mapping failures.
User interface issues.
Automation rule improvements.

This makes it easier for the development team to prioritize fixes.

Why This Was Important

Testing the dashboard before operational rollout prevents costly decision-making based on incorrect data.

The audit proved that some dashboard recommendations could have resulted in profitable campaigns being paused simply because incorrect revenue data was being displayed.

Business Impact

This validation protects both advertising budget and company revenue by ensuring automation decisions are based on reliable information.

The recommendations also improve long-term operational efficiency by replacing manual processes with automated workflows that save management time and reduce the risk of human error.
