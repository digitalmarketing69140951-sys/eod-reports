Task 2 – Performance Max ROAS Strategy Optimisation
Reviewed bidding performance for the campaign:
PMax UK | Sonya | Klarna | PH_ALL | Conversion > 2 | MCV | UK
Optimisation Completed
Target ROAS changed from 400% to 370%.
Why This Change Was Made
Campaign performance over the previous 30 days averaged approximately 330% ROAS, meaning the existing 400% Target ROAS had become too restrictive.
The higher bidding target was limiting Google's ability to compete effectively in auctions, reducing impression share and conversion opportunities.
Reducing the Target ROAS to 370% provides additional bidding flexibility while still maintaining a strong profitability target.
Expected Impact
Increase auction competitiveness.
Improve conversion volume.
Stabilise campaign delivery.
Create a healthier balance between sales growth and ROAS.
No additional bidding changes were required for other campaigns after reviewing their performance.
