* A practical Clarity URL/recording workflow was established.
* Two PDPs were optimised using actual traffic/conversion signals.

### What did not produce a final action

The leakage list did **not** result in live exclusions because Google Ads data alone was considered insufficient for the final decision and Clarity access is pending.

### Time efficiency issue

The leakage task was originally scoped as a product-exclusion activity but expanded into a **Clarity workflow investigation**. The revised planned allocation was **70 minutes**, with **65 minutes actually used**. This kept the work within the revised scope while avoiding premature live exclusions.

### Key lesson

High spend plus low/no conversion should be treated as a **candidate leakage signal**, not an automatic exclusion rule. Customer behaviour, PDP friction and purchase intent need to be differentiated before permanently removing products from campaign exposure.
