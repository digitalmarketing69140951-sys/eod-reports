# Diagnostic Reflection

### What was tried

* Validated the performance impact of previous PDP optimisation work.
* Reviewed inefficient products using spend, conversion, and eligibility filters.
* Completed campaign health assessment.
* Optimised two PDPs.
* Restored one product's Merchant Center eligibility.
* Implemented an approved campaign budget increase.

### What worked

* Visibility increased across several previously optimised products.
* Two inefficient products were removed from active optimisation.
* Merchant Center eligibility successfully restored for one product.
* Gems continued to demonstrate stronger conversion efficiency.
* Budget scaling was implemented through the correct approval process.

### What did not yet show measurable impact

* Several validated products require additional traffic before optimisation impact can be fully assessed.
* Low-traffic products remain statistically insignificant for optimisation decisions.

### Lessons Learned

* Validation is essential before attributing improvements to PDP changes.
* Eligibility issues can limit performance regardless of PDP quality.
* Statistical significance should guide optimisation and exclusion decisions.
* Campaign structural changes should follow documented approval and be monitored before further adjustments.
