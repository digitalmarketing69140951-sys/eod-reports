# The “Why” Analysis

## Campaign Recovery Logic

Campaign 1 performance deterioration was partially linked to accidental exclusion of a historically profitable SKU. Reinstating the E27 Plug-in Pendant restores a proven conversion anchor while maintaining removal of waste inventory.

## Traffic Quality Logic

Retailer-query exclusions successfully shifted traffic from broad comparison shoppers toward specialist industrial lighting intent. Lower CPC and higher conversion rate validated stronger auction positioning and better buyer alignment.

## PDP Optimization Logic

Technical clarity additions targeted key abandonment triggers:

* compatibility uncertainty
* installation fear
* UK setup concerns

This improves conversion confidence without requiring full PDP rewrites.

## Creative Strategy Logic

The “Shadow Test” shifts product positioning from hardware-focused presentation toward lifestyle and atmosphere-focused merchandising. Expected impact areas:

* CTR improvement
* engagement time increase
* perceived product value enhancement
