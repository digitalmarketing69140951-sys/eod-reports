## Diagnostic Reflection

### What was tried

* Weekly campaign performance analysis.
* Search term quality assessment.
* High CPC and zero-conversion product review.
* PDP optimisation with pricing review.
* Shopify catalogue expansion.

### What worked

* Weekly analysis clearly identified the performance gap between Mystery and Gems.
* Search term review uncovered irrelevant traffic and valuable keyword opportunities.
* Product-level review highlighted profitable products while identifying inefficient spend.
* PDP optimisation improved content quality, pricing competitiveness, and feed consistency.
* Two Merchant Center-ready listings were completed efficiently.

### What required continued monitoring

* Mystery campaign performance remains below the target ROAS.
* Fifty search terms require additional performance data before further action.
* Recommended pause/exclusion products should be validated after approval and monitoring.

### Lessons Learned

* Weekly analysis provides stronger optimisation direction than relying on daily metrics alone.
* Variant-level evaluation prevents profitable products from being excluded because of weaker variants.
* Combining search term analysis with PDP optimisation strengthens both Shopping feed quality and future campaign relevance.
* Maintaining a consistent target of **2 listings per day** supports catalogue growth while preserving time for higher-priority Google Ads optimisation.
