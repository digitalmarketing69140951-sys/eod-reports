# 4. Why Analysis

### What worked

**Performance-first workflow:**
The first 35-minute task established the current campaign-level performance before making waste-control decisions.

**Evidence-based product review:**
The exclusion review identified **£81.78 of spend across 178 clicks with zero conversions/value** using a defined screening rule.

**Search-term segmentation:**
The search-term review distinguished between:

* Clearly irrelevant traffic → **Negative**
* Relevant but limited evidence → **Monitor**
* Relevant traffic requiring additional conversion opportunity → **Keep**

### What failed / risks

The main risk is **over-exclusion** if product or search-term decisions are expanded beyond the documented evidence.

The 15-product list has strong non-conversion evidence under the supplied threshold, but exclusion should remain a controlled action rather than assuming every non-converting SKU is permanently poor-performing.

Similarly, the search-term review contained only **4 terms**, so it should be treated as a focused monitoring sample rather than a complete account-wide search-term audit.

A three-stage half-day workflow was completed: campaign performance analysis → product waste screening → search-term quality monitoring.

**What failed:**
No task failure was recorded. However, the search-term review was limited to four identified terms and should not be treated as a complete account-wide search-term audit.

* Performance analysis: **5 min**

**Lesson learned:**
Maintaining explicit evidence thresholds helps distinguish actionable waste from products/search terms that simply have insufficient conversion data.
