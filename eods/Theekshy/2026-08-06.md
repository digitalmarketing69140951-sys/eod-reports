PDP optimisation was directly informed by campaign performance data. One product represented a proven high-performing asset suitable for continued optimisation, while the second product addressed issues identified during the leakage analysis.

# Diagnostic Reflection

## What was tried

* Campaign-level performance review.
* Product-level leakage analysis.
* Evidence-based PDP optimisation.
* Multi-variant listing transfer and verification.

## What worked

* Product-level analysis clearly identified where campaign spend was concentrated.
* Data-driven product selection ensured optimisation work aligned with campaign findings.
* Product transfer maintained consistent metadata and variant quality.

## What did not work

* **THEE_GEMS** continued to record low ROAS (**63.30%**) during the review period.
* A high proportion of spend remained concentrated among products without recorded conversions.

## Lessons Learned

* Product-level analysis provides clearer optimisation priorities than campaign-level metrics alone.
* Linking implementation tasks directly to analytical findings creates a stronger optimisation workflow.
* High-spend products should be monitored over an appropriate evaluation period before exclusion decisions are made.
