## Diagnostic Reflection

**What was tried:**
Google Ads performance screening, Microsoft Clarity behaviour validation, controlled product exclusion, PMax asset review, and two evidence-led PDP optimisations.

**What worked:**
Clarity allowed the zero-conversion product pool to be segmented rather than treated as uniformly wasted spend.

**What failed / limitation:**
Not all products had sufficient Clarity sample volume. **9 SKUs** lacked sufficient behavioural data, so exclusion could not be justified.

**Where time was controlled:**
The exclusion implementation required only **6 minutes** because there was only one validated candidate. No unnecessary mass exclusion or extensive creative redesign was performed.

**Lesson:**
Zero conversion is a **screening signal, not an exclusion rule**. Behavioural evidence should determine whether the next action is exclusion, PDP optimisation, feed improvement or continued testing.
