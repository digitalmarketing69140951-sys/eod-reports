## Product optimisation control

Only **2 SKUs** were optimised, addressing **£5.25 historical spend** and **6 clicks**. This avoided spending the entire 60-minute allocation on broad product editing.

## Account-holder request

The duplicate-price issue was a legitimate feed/commerce-data dependency because inconsistent duplicate prices can create product-level competitiveness and feed-quality problems. The task was capped at **30 minutes** and completed in **25 minutes**, preventing it from expanding into an unrestricted duplicate-product audit.

## Evidence limitation

The product optimisation sample was small:

* SKU 1: **4 clicks**
* SKU 2: **2 clicks**

Therefore, post-change performance needs to validate whether the PDP/title changes improve conversion efficiency.

Similarly, the duplicate-price evidence identifies mismatches but does not by itself establish that every Compare Price should be changed. Shopify's actual selling price remains the appropriate reference for final correction.
