**What was tried:**
SKU-level performance analysis, proportional high-spend filtering, click-engagement screening, targeted PDP intervention and historical converting search-term integration.

**What worked:**
Campaign efficiency improved materially versus the previous baseline. THEE_GEMS reached **243.56% ROAS**, while both campaigns showed lower Cost/Conversion.

**What failed / remains unproven:**
The 34 exclusion recommendations and two PDP changes have not yet generated measurable post-change results. Future performance must validate whether the interventions reduce leakage and improve conversion efficiency.

**Where time was controlled:**
No unnecessary catalogue-wide optimisation was performed. The planned **215 minutes** of work was completed in **175 minutes**.

**Lesson:**
The current workflow is more efficient when leakage analysis directly determines which products are excluded and which are protected for PDP intervention.
