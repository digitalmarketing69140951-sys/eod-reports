## The "Why" Analysis

Today's analysis focused on validating whether Campaign 2's reduced ROAS was caused by feed segmentation issues or product-level inefficiencies.

The evidence indicates:

* Feed segmentation is functioning correctly.
* Conversion volume increased substantially after segmentation.
* CPA remained stable despite increased spend.
* No individual Gems products demonstrated sufficient wasted spend to justify exclusion.
* The £10.75 identified across the top three non-converting products is too small to explain the reduction from 500.54% ROAS to 319.02% ROAS.

Current evidence suggests that Campaign 2's lower ROAS is more likely associated with spend scaling and traffic expansion than structural campaign issues.

* Feed segmentation should continue to be evaluated using sufficient post-change data before intervention.
