## Diagnostic Reflection

**What was tried:**
Campaign-level performance analysis, competitor/retailer brand exclusions, evidence-based negative keywords, two product-level PDP optimisations and an urgent free-shipping asset correction.

**What worked:**
The full planned workload was completed within individual time caps. Traffic-quality actions were based on actual non-converting search/brand evidence. PDP work remained limited to two products.

**What failed / remains unproven:**
No causal ROAS improvement can yet be attributed to today's brand exclusions, negative keywords or PDP changes because sufficient post-change data is not available.

**Where time was saved:**
Daily Performance Analysis finished **10 min under plan** and Negative Keyword Addition **6 min under plan**. The unplanned asset task also finished **5 min under its 15-minute allowance**.

**Lessons learned:**
Campaign-level traffic-quality controls should remain evidence-based, while PDP and creative work should remain tightly scoped. Same-day changes to commercial offers require immediate asset consistency checks to prevent outdated messaging.
