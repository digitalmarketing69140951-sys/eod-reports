Diagnostic Reflection
What was tried
Investigated search inconsistencies by comparing predictive search with full search behaviour.
Traced Merchant Center errors back through Shopify sales channel configuration.
Reviewed historical Shopify events to reconstruct product modification history.
Audited previous theme modifications to identify missing search UI.
Redesigned the 404 experience while maintaining theme compatibility and accessibility.
What worked
Root-cause analysis identified hidden Liquid conditions instead of symptoms.
Restoring commented-out functionality resolved the missing search input without affecting search logic.
Merchant Center issue was confirmed to be configuration-related rather than indexing-related.
The 404 redesign remained fully self-contained with no JavaScript dependency.
What failed and why
Google Merchant Center validation cannot be completed immediately because feed processing depends on Google's re-crawl schedule.
Shopify's native event system cannot recover deleted variant history or provide field-level modification records.
Where time was wasted
Investigating Merchant Center indexing before discovering the sales channel exclusion.
Attempting deeper historical variant attribution beyond Shopify's native audit capabilities.
What will change tomorrow
Verify Merchant Center diagnostics after the re-crawl completes.
Prepare deployment of completed theme fixes.
Push validated theme changes to Git.
Continue documenting reusable debugging patterns for future incidents.
Lessons learned / Actionable Next Steps
Predictive search should mirror search result logic to prevent inconsistent customer experiences.
Always verify Shopify sales channel publication before investigating Merchant Center indexing.
During theme rewrites, ensure essential UI components are not unintentionally removed inside Liquid comment blocks.
Capture reusable troubleshooting patterns immediately after validation to support knowledge compounding.

Should predictive search intentionally include out-of-stock products across all LEDsone stores as the standard behaviour?
