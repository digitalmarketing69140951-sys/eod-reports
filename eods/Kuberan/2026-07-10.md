# Diagnostic Reflection

### What was tried

* Rebuilt Mahima Requirement 1 completely instead of incrementally modifying the previous implementation.
* Improved Google Ads data matching through join optimization.
* Expanded historical reporting for Search Terms.
* Updated dashboard index information after report deployments.
* Enhanced campaign reporting usability for Thasitha.

### What worked

* Full rebuild produced a cleaner and more maintainable implementation.
* Resolving case-sensitive joins significantly improved product matching accuracy.
* Validation against Google Ads UI confirmed report accuracy.
* Extended historical reporting provided more comprehensive analysis.
* Dashboard statistics remained synchronized after deployment.

### What failed and why

* Initial join logic produced lower product matching due to case sensitivity.
* Real Status information remained limited because source data currently provides only partial coverage (11.6%).

### Where time was wasted

* Additional debugging was required to identify the case-sensitive join issue.
* Time was spent validating multiple deployment environments to ensure consistent production updates.

### What will change tomorrow

* Continue improving remaining unmatched product coverage where source data allows.
* Reuse the rebuilt architecture as the standard for future reporting modules.
* Continue expanding standardized dashboard components.

### Lessons learned / Actionable next steps

* Complete rebuilds can be more efficient than extensive incremental modifications.
* Cross-validation against production interfaces greatly improves confidence before deployment.
* Database join consistency should be verified early during development.
* Standardized dashboard components reduce future maintenance effort.

### Questions for STL / Team Leader / LLM

* Should the rebuilt Mahima Requirement 1 architecture become the template for future Product Performance reports?
* Can standardized badge and toolbar components be extracted into reusable dashboard modules?
* Should additional Google Ads datasets be integrated to improve Status coverage beyond the current 11.6%?
