CUSTOM-INTEREST AUDIENCE SEGMENT
Segment Name

LEDsone – Competitor Shopper Lookalike (Lighting)

The segment was given a clear and descriptive name so that it can be identified and audited easily in future reporting.

The existing audience segment named “Sneaky” should be reviewed before deployment to confirm that it does not overlap with the new segment.

Strategic Reason

Existing Google in-market audiences already show strong natural relevance:

Power & Electrical Supplies: 37.9x audience index
Lights & Fixtures: 31.9x audience index

These results indicate that Google’s standard in-market categories are already identifying relevant users effectively.

The custom-interest segment is therefore intended to act as an additional audience signal rather than replace or restrict the existing in-market audiences.

This follows the campaign principle of:

Layer audience signals without restricting campaign delivery.

The new segment adds competitor-shopping behaviour, product-interest signals and high-intent search behaviour that may not be fully represented within Google’s standard audience categories.
