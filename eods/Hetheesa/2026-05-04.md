## 6️⃣ Diagnostic Reflection (Mandatory)

### GSC Triage

* **What was tried:** Identified declining pages and applied meta + internal link fixes
* **What worked:** Quick optimization improved page relevance
* **What failed and why:** No deep query-level analysis done before fix
* **Where time was wasted:** Switching between pages without structured checklist
* **What will change tomorrow:** Follow strict diagnosis (query / CTR / position) before fixing
* **Lessons learned:** Fixes without root cause reduce effectiveness
* **Questions:** Which metric dropped most (CTR vs position) for each page?

* **Lessons learned:** Documentation is as important as execution
