The "Why" Analysis

The day's work balanced campaign optimisation with long-term feed improvements. Lowering the Topsell Target ROAS is a reasonable response to underdelivery, as it can make the campaign more competitive in auctions. However, because the campaign still reports a limited audience, the underlying issue is likely related to audience reach, listing eligibility, or campaign structure rather than bidding alone.

One item that deserves immediate follow-up is the €18.36 in sales from excluded products. This suggests some exclusions may now be preventing revenue-generating products from participating. Before re-enabling them, verify whether the sales were attributed after exclusion (for example, due to delayed conversion attribution) or whether the products are genuinely performing again. If performance has improved consistently, reassess those exclusions using a broader date range and profitability criteria rather than a single day's data.

The Add-to-Cart investigation is also a valuable next step. Since users are reaching the cart but not purchasing, the problem may lie further down the funnel. Focus on:

Checkout abandonment in GA4.
Shipping fees and delivery messaging.
Payment method availability.
Mobile checkout experience.
Page speed during checkout.
Trust signals such as reviews, returns policy, and payment security.
Comparison of abandoned products versus purchased products to identify recurring patterns.
