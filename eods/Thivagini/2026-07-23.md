The "Why" Analysis

Today's optimisation focused on increasing campaign flexibility and improving inventory quality. Lowering the Target ROAS for Imp_Click aligns with the observed under-delivery and very low CPC, giving Google's bidding algorithm more room to compete in auctions. The expansion of Search Themes should also help Performance Max discover additional relevant search intent over time.

However, the recurring pattern of zero recorded conversion value across both campaigns remains the highest-priority issue. Continued feed optimisation and listing expansion are valuable, but they are unlikely to restore ROAS if the underlying constraint is inaccurate conversion measurement or friction after the ad click. The next optimisation cycle should concentrate on validating conversion tracking, reviewing attribution against Shopify, and analysing checkout abandonment to determine why engaged users are not completing purchases.
