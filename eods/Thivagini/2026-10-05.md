What failed / risk identified

Google also reported that the campaign had not generated conversion value during the past week.
Given the previously observed Google Ads/Shopify conversion-value discrepancies, the zero reported value should not automatically be interpreted as zero actual sales without checking backend/Shopify attribution.

Where time was potentially wasted

The main efficiency risk is spending time optimizing bidding or campaign recommendations before resolving the underlying conversion-value reliability issue.

What will change tomorrow

Continue validating Google Ads conversion reporting against actual orders before making aggressive budget or bidding changes.
Monitor the UK campaign after the 230% Target ROAS change rather than making another immediate bid adjustment.

Lessons learned / actionable next steps

Bid strategy changes should be evaluated against both reported Google Ads conversion value and validated actual sales.
Campaigns with serving restrictions should have their diagnostics investigated before increasing budgets.
The strongest immediate efficiency opportunity remains controlling spend from campaigns/products that are not producing validated sales.

Questions for STL / Team Leader / LLM

Should the 230% Target ROAS on the slow-moving UK campaign be retained for a defined observation period before further changes?
Should the FR Imp_Click campaign's conversion tracking be investigated before applying additional optimization recommendations?
