What Failed and Why
Google Ads conversion tracking is severely under-reporting revenue.

Where Time Was Wasted
Considerable analysis effort was required because platform reporting does not match Shopify sales data.
What Will Change Tomorrow
Investigate Shopify conversion tag implementation.
Audit enhanced conversions and purchase event firing.
Validate Google Ads conversion action settings.
Compare GA4, Shopify, and Google Ads attribution paths.
Reassess Smart Bidding after tracking corrections.
Lessons Learned
Product exclusions should never be based solely on Google Ads reported conversions.
Shopify order validation must be part of every product-level audit.
Tracking integrity directly impacts Smart Bidding performance and ROAS optimisation.
Questions for STL / Team Leader / LLM
When was the last tracking implementation update performed?
Are Enhanced Conversions properly configured?
Is purchase value being passed consistently?
Should bidding strategy changes be paused until tracking is fixed?
Should hidden-revenue products receive separate segmentation once attribution is restored?
