What worked

What failed and why
Increasing tROAS on low-performing campaigns
→ Further restricts spend and delays learning
Skipping validation before scaling
→ Decisions made without confirming trend direction
Where time was wasted
Simultaneous execution of too many strategic changes
→ Reduced ability to isolate impact of each action
What will change tomorrow
Prioritize post-change validation before further optimization
Avoid stacking multiple major changes in one cycle
Focus on unlocking spend in constrained campaigns instead of tightening
Lessons learned / actionable next steps
Scaling should follow validation, not precede it
tROAS is a constraint lever — not just a performance target
Feed optimization is a compounding asset and should remain consistent
