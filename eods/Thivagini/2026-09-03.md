## 6. Diagnostic Reflection

### Campaign Performance Review

**What was tried:**

* Reviewed all three active PMax campaigns using the latest available campaign data.
* Compared Google Ads conversion-value signals with backend-adjusted sales where available.
* Checked budget, serving status, optimization score and delivery signals.

**What worked:**

* Topsell continues to show stronger underlying performance when backend sales are considered.
* New Arrivals has a high optimization score and is technically eligible, although it remains in Learning.

**What failed and why:**

* Google Ads conversion-value reporting does not fully align with backend sales for Topsell.
* New Arrivals generated no recorded conversion value during the reviewed day and has limited audience availability while learning.
* Imp_Click also recorded zero conversion value for the reviewed day.

**Where time was potentially wasted:**

* Repeated manual reconciliation of Google Ads and backend sales remains necessary because of the conversion-value discrepancy.
* Further optimization of New Arrivals should be avoided until sufficient learning data is available.

**What will change tomorrow:**

* Continue monitoring the three campaigns rather than making multiple simultaneous structural changes.
* Prioritize conversion tracking/reconciliation for campaigns where backend sales materially differ from Google Ads values.
* Evaluate the impact of the Topsell tROAS increase before making another bidding adjustment.

**Lessons learned / actionable next steps:**

* Do not judge campaign profitability solely from Google Ads reported ROAS when backend sales demonstrate a material tracking gap.
* Avoid rapid optimization of New Arrivals during the Learning phase.
* Treat Topsell as an efficiency-protection case rather than an immediate scaling case until the effect of the 276% tROAS target is validated.
* Continue monitoring spend against actual sales to identify wasted spend.

**Questions for STL / Team Leader / LLM:**

* Should conversion tracking be audited further to reduce the Google Ads vs backend conversion-value gap?
* Should Topsell remain at 276% tROAS until additional post-change data is available?
* What threshold should be used before increasing the New Arrivals budget or changing its bidding strategy?
