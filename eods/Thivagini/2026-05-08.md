6️⃣ Diagnostic Reflection
Weekly ROAS Analysis


What was tried: Full campaign-level ROAS review


What worked: Clearly identified strongest and weakest campaigns


What failed and why: Best Sellers continued weak performance with audience restrictions


Where time was wasted: Limited earlier focus on contribution-level analysis


What will change tomorrow: Shift budget and focus toward proven profitable campaigns


Lessons learned: Blended ROAS can hide weak campaign efficiency


Questions: Should Best Sellers budget be fully reallocated to Topsell?



Waste Reduction Validation


What was tried: Search term, landing page, and placement analysis


What worked: Identified clear spend leakage and low-intent traffic sources


What failed and why: Cheap CPC traffic produced weak conversion quality


Where time was wasted: Broad traffic acquisition without purchase intent filtering


What will change tomorrow: Expand negative keyword strategy aggressively


Lessons learned: Low CPC is meaningless without conversion quality


Questions: Which search themes repeatedly generate low-intent clicks?



SKU Exclusion & Placement Cleanup


What was tried: Excluded high-cost non-converting products and placements


What worked: Reduced inefficient spend allocation


What failed and why: Some exclusions occurred after significant spend accumulation


Where time was wasted: Delayed SKU-level profitability review


What will change tomorrow: Build earlier detection process for waste products


Lessons learned: Product-level monitoring is essential in PMax


Questions: What spend threshold should trigger automatic exclusion review?



Landing & Collection Page Findings


What was tried: Evaluated landing page performance quality


What worked: Identified low-performing collection and discount pages


What failed and why: Collection pages drove traffic without purchase behavior


Where time was wasted: Sending paid traffic to browse-oriented pages


What will change tomorrow: Prioritize product-specific landing pages


Lessons learned: Product intent pages outperform generic collections


Questions: Should collection URLs be excluded from final URL expansion?



Feed Optimization & Fine Tuning


What was tried: Product title, description, image, and pricing improvements


What worked: Improved feed quality and listing readiness


What failed and why: Impact on conversion rate not validated yet


Where time was wasted: Broad optimization without direct profitability prioritization earlier


What will change tomorrow: Prioritize only high-potential SKUs


Lessons learned: Feed optimization should follow profitability signals


Questions: Which optimized SKUs are gaining CTR improvement?
